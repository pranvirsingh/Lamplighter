## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-02

The first public release.

### New
- A wordless point-and-click adventure: guide Wick, a little tin lamplighter, through a town gone dark
- Six hand-drawn scenes, from Wick's workshop through the market, the canal, the hill and the square to the clock tower
- Light the four lamps by finding and using items, helping the townsfolk and their critters, and solving machine puzzles
- Four machine puzzles on close-up cards (a pressure valve, a gear train, mirrors and a light beam, glyph rings), plus a telescope that shows a clue
- Tap Wick any time for a picture hint of what to do next
- Fireflies to catch for your jar, and a sepia and charcoal world where amber light is the only colour
- Synthesised music and sound effects, each with its own on/off switch
